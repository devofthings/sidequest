# Sidequest vocabulary

Shared language for the mixed reading and playing experience.

## Language

**Feed**: The ordered stream of news cards and game cards a person moves through.

**News card**: A feed item presenting a short news story with its source and a link to the original article.
_Avoid_: News game, article game.

**Game card**: A feed item containing one playable activity.
_Avoid_: News card when referring to playable content.

**Challenge**: One bounded puzzle or game objective within a game card.

**Session**: One visit to Sidequest in which a person reads or plays through the feed.

**Source article**: The original publisher's story underlying a news card.

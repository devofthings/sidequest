# Sidequest competitor research

Research date: **October 3, 2026**. Prepared for [issue #11](https://github.com/devofthings/sidequest/issues/11).

Sidequest is testing whether a shuffled feed of sourced English news and quick games feels enjoyable enough to reopen. Its confirmed game scope remains mini Sudoku, memory pairs, one-move chess puzzles, and a bubble shooter. This research proposes design questions; it does not change accepted MVP decisions.

## What the evidence establishes

Swipeable game feeds already exist: Reel Games, Kracked, Auren Brain Puzzle, and Yes advertise that interaction. News and puzzles also already coexist inside publisher apps, including the Guardian and the New York Times, and inside Apple News. The opportunity to test is narrower: playable games and short, attributed news interleaved in one manually controlled feed. This is an inference from the comparisons below, not proof of an unoccupied market. [Reel Games](https://www.reelgamesapp.com/), [Kracked](https://www.kracked.app/), [Brain Puzzle](https://www.aurenapps.com/auren-brain-puzzle/), [Yes](https://www.playonyes.com/), [Guardian App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-guardian-us-world-news/id409128287), [NYT Connections help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/360011158491-New-York-Times-Games/28525912587924-Connections), [Apple News puzzle navigation](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/find-puzzles-in-apple-news-iph4883822da/ios).

- **Auren:** website calls the product iPhone-only; its linked listing also supports iPad. Listing description retains the older Wonderscroll name. The publisher's website and listing are evidence of claims, not independent evidence that training improves general cognition. ## Design handoff: proposed questions for the design decision

1. **Make the mixed-feed test explicit.** Existing products cover both ingredients. Show a news card followed by a playable card in the initial prototype and test whether switching itself feels valuable. Apple already places puzzle entry points in its Today feed; Sidequest should demonstrate the experience of playing directly among news cards. This is an inference from [Apple's documented navigation](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/find-puzzles-in-apple-news-iph4883822da/ios).
2. **Use one recognizable card frame with distinct content.** Proposed: a stable title/type area and consistent next-card cue, while news emphasizes headline, source, publication time, and excerpt, and games emphasize the board and a short objective. This preserves the accepted mixed feed and attribution requirements; it is a Sidequest design proposal.
3. **Design for gesture ownership and the accepted reset rule.** Bubble aiming and chess moves must not accidentally advance the feed. Give the feed gesture a clear region or threshold, test it on an iPhone, and communicate that skipping ends an unfinished attempt. Reel Games advertises returning to prior progress, so Sidequest's different confirmed rule deserves deliberate usability testing. [Reel Games](https://www.reelgamesapp.com/).
4. **Keep the first game set.** Sudoku and chess overlap with Reel Games; Auren demonstrates memory-sequence challenges. The research does not justify adding a large catalog or replacing memory pairs with sequence memory. Validate the four accepted games as short, understandable rounds; do not infer mechanic equivalence from product names. [Reel Games](https://www.reelgamesapp.com/), [Brain Puzzle](https://www.aurenapps.com/auren-brain-puzzle/).
5. **Evaluate a satisfying stopping point as a later option.** Auren advertises session-ending summaries, providing an alternative to endless-discovery positioning. Propose it for design discussion rather than silently adding a session limit, streaks, or daily quotas to the MVP. [Brain Puzzle](https://www.aurenapps.com/auren-brain-puzzle/).

For a later installed-app check, compare the first 60 seconds, control discoverability, what happens after swiping away mid-game, whether returning restores an attempt, and how puzzles/news are discovered. Record observed behavior separately from this advertised-feature snapshot.

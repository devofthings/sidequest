# Pocket-game specification

Read [the selected specification](README.md) and [#117](https://github.com/devofthings/sidequest/issues/117) before changing compact variants. The owner selected its recommended first revision on October 5, 2026: fixed 4×4 Chess focus windows over standard-board fixtures; originally retain 4×4 Sudoku, six-pair Memory, and small Bubble challenges. Later owner-approved #162/#170 supersedes mini-only production with standard 9×9 Sudoku, at most 12 editable cells and full-size coordinate alternatives; the original mini audit remains historical. Harder Bubble and controls follow #137/#138/#163. Active-play ranges are unmeasured usability targets, never timers. [#132](https://github.com/devofthings/sidequest/issues/132) owns Chess implementation; difficulty #118 and adaptive Idea #119 remain separate.

Chess presentation retains standard 8×8 coordinates and independently validated complete legal/mate sets. Freeze the window per challenge, display all pieces and accepted destinations, and preserve full-board coordinate input. A focus-window edge never becomes a rule boundary. Unsupported future fixtures must use a full-board presentation instead of silently clipping information; no new fixture or rule system is authorized.

This area owns the selected specification and its supporting evidence. Keep original audit evidence distinct from new rendered verification. Preserve explicit start, frozen challenges, unfinished reset, completed revisits, accessibility, and stable geometry under shared guidance.

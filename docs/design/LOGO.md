### Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one original iPhone app icon concept for Sidequest, a private app mixing short news stories with Sudoku, memory, chess puzzles, and a bubble shooter in a shuffled swipe feed. Create a simple memorable symbol suggesting curiosity and a small branching side path, subtly combining two stacked cards with a playful route. Minimal crisp vector-like geometry, flat restrained colors selected for clarity, strong negative space, readable at tiny home-screen sizes. A single centered mark on an opaque square background filling the image edge to edge. No text, no letters, no watermark, no mockup, no phone, no outer rounded-corner mask, no copied game or publisher logos. This is a review concept, not a finalized brand direction.
